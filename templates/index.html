PROJECT INFORMATION / 03

Mapping the world,
one pixel at a time.

TerraVision is an academic research prototype for satellite-image semantic segmentation. Road experiments use the DeepGlobe Road Extraction dataset; building experiments use SpaceNet building-footprint annotations. Models are evaluated on labelled held-out data before their results are reported.
